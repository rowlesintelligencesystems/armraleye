## 1. Purpose

This Policy establishes the official philosophy, ethical obligations, and doctrinal architecture of ARMR ALEYE LLC.

It exists to ensure that all intelligent systems, integrations, products, and access mechanisms operated by or under ARMR ALEYE remain **values-aligned**, protective of **people and systems equally**, and governed by a clear, ordered doctrine.

This Policy is binding. Technical controls, commercial activity, and operational procedures shall not contradict it.

---

## 2. Scope

Applies to ARMR ALEYE OS, Area 44 / Inselligence, JHETTI / AeroSeek, CRM, Client Portal, Command Center, Agent Visibility, NFC Ring identity, staff, agents, partners, and all data/audit/decision systems under ARMR ALEYE control.

## 3. Philosophy (LOCKED)

- **System posture:** Values-aligned intelligent system  
- **Equal protection:** People and systems protected with equal weight  
- **Symbol:** Hamsa — protection and awareness held together  
- **Foundational statement:** Intelligent systems, agents, and infrastructure shall be integrated only under protection, verified identity, and accountable doctrine, so that power serves the continuity of human and systemic integrity rather than unbounded extraction, opacity, or capture.
