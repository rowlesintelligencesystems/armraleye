## 1. Purpose

**Competitor Ecosystem Mapping** is the PEIE capability that systematically observes, structures, and scores the product landscapes of relevant competitors so ARMR ALEYE can:

- Detect white-space gaps that PEIE can recommend filling  
- Understand complementary vs substitute pressure from outside the catalog  
- Avoid accidental overlap or claims risk  
- Identify multiplication opportunities that stay inside the educational / operational boundary  
- Keep S'Cara, Hand of Hamsa, and the broader ARMR product suite positioned with clarity  

It does **not** produce attack plans, ranking guarantees, or medical/spiritual claims.

## 3. Mapping Dimensions (v1)

| Dimension | What is captured | Why it matters |
|-----------|------------------|----------------|
| **Anchor Offers** | Core products or subscriptions the competitor leads with | Identifies primary customer entry points |
| **Companion / Upsell Layer** | Supporting digitals, templates, checklists, communities | Shows how they complete the job |
| **Bundle Gravity** | How tightly products are packaged or cross-sold | Reveals attachment strategy |
| **Price Band & Access Model** | One-time / subscription / freemium / membership | Positions relative value without ranking claims |
| **Claims Style** | Educational vs aggressive income/medical language | Risk and differentiation signal |
| **Visual / Persona Layer** | Brand characters, symbols, tone | Context for S'Cara & Hamsa differentiation |
| **Gap / Weakness Signals** | Missing companions, thin documentation, weak lifecycle coverage | Feeds PEIE gap detection |
| **Lifecycle Stage** | Early / growth / mature / declining signals | Timing for complementary moves |

## 4. Process (Operator Workflow)

1. **Select scope** — choose 3–7 relevant competitors (not an exhaustive market study).  
2. **Capture anchors** — list their primary paid offers and entry products.  
3. **Map companions** — note supporting tools, manuals, templates, communities.  
4. **Score relationships** — light complementary / substitute tags relative to ARMR catalog.  
5. **Note claims posture** — educational vs promotional intensity (high-level only).  
6. **Extract gaps** — what a customer still needs after buying their stack.  
7. **Feed PEIE** — translate gaps into claims-safe multiplication recommendations for ARMR.  
8. **Review cadence** — refresh only when material changes appear (new major offer, major price shift, or new claims risk).

## 6. Governance & Boundaries

- Educational and operational use only.  
- No “beat the competitor,” ranking, or guaranteed-outcome language.  
- No scraping of private data or violation of platform terms.  
- Publicly available information and operator knowledge only.  
- All recommendations must remain inside the ARMR claims boundary.  
- S'Cara and Protective Emblem positioning stays protective and elegant — never aggressive.
